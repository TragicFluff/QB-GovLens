# Writing the case review report

The report is a review aid, not a finding. A human administrator decides what the pattern means and what happens next.

## You must not

- flag, re-tier or re-score the case, or say it should or should not have been flagged;
- say or imply a breach, fraud, collusion, policy violation or an obligation to run an ITQ;
- guess a person's intent or identity, or name anyone beyond the buyer labels given;
- introduce facts or passages that the analyst or researcher did not provide.

## Structure (use these headings, in this order)

1. **Signal**: one or two sentences on what the rule found, in plain words.
2. **Transaction evidence**: the facts that matter most, each with `[QB:order_id]` citations. Lead with the strongest fact either way.
3. **Relevant guidance**: what the cited passages say about this kind of pattern, each claim with its passage citation.
4. **Possible legitimate explanations**: grounded in the evidence or the guidance (e.g. a seller limiting quantity per cart, separate teams restocking a pantry).
5. **Checks for the administrator**: two to four concrete questions or documents to ask for.

## Style

Plain English, short sentences, under about 350 words. Use "the officer" or the buyer labels, not names. Write amounts as $1,234.56. End with one sentence saying this is a review list item, not a finding.
